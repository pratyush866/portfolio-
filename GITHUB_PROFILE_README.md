## 👨‍💻 Executive Mission & About Me

I am a Technology Founder and Full-Stack Architect from **Khalilabad, Sant Kabir Nagar (Uttar Pradesh), India**. I engineer sovereign digital trade ecosystems, vernacular AI integrations, and zero-brokerage architectures that empower grassroots Indian communities — from agricultural farmers to matrimonial families and intercity drivers.

- 🌾 **Founder & CEO at Kisan Market (किसान मार्केट):** India's premier 0% commission direct agricultural commodity trade exchange with Sarvam AI Indian vernacular voice navigation, FastAPI microservices, and Razorpay escrow.
- 🕉️ **Lead Architect of Shaadi Ram Ghar Jode (शादी राम घर जोड़े):** Authentic Vedic Matrimony and certified Gurukul Purohit booking ecosystem featuring a 36-Guna Ashtakoota Milan engine.
- 🚕 **Founder of Travel Market (ट्रैवल मार्केट):** 100% toll-inclusive intercity cab exchange and direct driver trust network built on React 19 and Supabase PostgreSQL.
- 🏅 **Honors & Credentials:** 13 verified credentials, including **A++ Grade Medalist** in Data Analytics Industrial Training (Techpile MSME) and leadership awards from ITM GIDA Gorakhpur.
- 💬 **Ask me about:** Full-stack architecture, Python FastAPI, Next.js, PostgreSQL/Supabase, vernacular AI voice systems, and AgriTech trade mechanics.
